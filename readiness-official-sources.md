## 2026–2027 ICDC milestone

Blue Blazer’s 2026–2027 readiness calendar uses the official DECA International Career Development Conference milestone of **April 17–20, 2027 in Anaheim, California**. The direct DECA conference page identifies the High School conference dates and location, while the DECA calendar page lists the April 17, 2027 start date.

- https://www.deca.org/conferences/icdc
- https://www.deca.org/calendar/deca-international-career-development-conference

The stored milestone is a planning anchor only. It does not represent qualification status or guarantee eligibility to attend ICDC.
