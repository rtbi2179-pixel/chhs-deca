# Practice Cluster Cards Browser Validation

On 2026-08-14, the authenticated Practice entry route displayed four dark-theme card launchers for Marketing, Business, Finance, and Hospitality & Tourism. Each card showed a Lucide icon, cluster description, question-bank volume, progress bar, and directional launch affordance inside the established Blue Blazer slate-and-blue visual system.

Selecting the Marketing card opened the existing filtered question bank with Marketing selected in the header, an all-difficulty filter, and 9,750 matching questions. The successful handoff confirmed that the new card launcher preserves the existing practice question-bank behavior.

The Marketing card was also previewed in its hover state. Its raised surface, elevated layered inset panels, accented border, and directional control use a short transform-only transition, providing the requested subtle lift without changing the page’s dark blue visual language.
