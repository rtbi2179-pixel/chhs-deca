# CHHS DECA Website — Design Brainstorm

## Three Design Approaches

<response>
<idea>
**Design Movement:** Neo-Brutalist Command Center
**Core Principles:**
- Hard-edged grid with deliberate asymmetry — information architecture as visual art
- Raw typographic power: oversized headers collide with tight data tables
- Monochromatic blue-black base punctuated by electric accent flashes
- Every element earns its place; zero decorative filler

**Color Philosophy:** Deep navy (#0a0f1e) as the void, electric cobalt (#1d4ed8) as the pulse, pure white for text hierarchy, and a single gold (#f59e0b) accent for ICDC achievement moments. The palette communicates authority and ambition.

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric split-column grid. Left rail is a persistent vertical nav (dark, narrow). Right content area uses a masonry-style irregular grid. Hero section is a full-bleed split: left half is text/data, right half is the 3D Spline glass object.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thick 2px electric-blue border lines as section dividers (not full borders, just partial edges)
- Monospaced counter numbers for stats (e.g., "60 EVENTS", "4 CLUSTERS")
- Glitch-hover effect on nav items

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interactions are fast and decisive — no slow fades. Hover states snap. Click states compress. The UI responds like a professional tool, not a consumer app.

**Animation:** Entrance animations use a staggered slide-up with 40ms delays. Hover on cards: border color flash + slight Y-lift. The 3D glass rotates on mouse proximity. Section transitions use a horizontal wipe.

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Space Grotesk" Bold 700 — geometric, technical authority
- Body: "DM Sans" Regular 400 — clean, highly readable
- Accent/labels: "JetBrains Mono" — monospaced for data and codes
</idea>

<idea>
**Design Movement:** Cinematic Dark Editorial
**Core Principles:**
- Deep cinematic darkness with carefully sculpted light — the DECA glass is the light source
- Editorial typography with extreme size contrast (massive display vs. small captions)
- Motion as narrative: the page tells a story as you scroll
- Luxury brand aesthetics applied to academic achievement

**Color Philosophy:** Near-black (#050810) background, midnight blue (#0d1b3e) for cards/sections, electric blue (#3b82f6) for interactive elements, and crisp white (#f8fafc) for text. The color story is "deep space with a beacon of blue light."

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-viewport sections with parallax depth. Each section is its own cinematic frame. The hero is a full-screen dark canvas with the 3D glass centered and glowing. Navigation floats as a translucent top bar with backdrop blur.

**Signature Elements:**
- Glowing blue halos/rings around key elements (the glass, section headers)
- Thin horizontal rule lines with gradient fade-out
- Particle field background on the hero section

**Interaction Philosophy:** Smooth, deliberate, and premium. Hover reveals are slow and graceful. Scroll triggers are tied to Framer Motion viewport detection. The experience feels like entering an elite organization.

**Animation:** Hero glass has idle float animation. Scroll-triggered section reveals use fade+scale. Navigation items have underline-grow hover. Cards have a subtle inner-glow pulse on hover.

**Typography System:**
- Display: "Bebas Neue" — tall, commanding, cinematic
- Body: "Outfit" Regular/Medium — modern, approachable
- Data labels: "Space Mono" — technical precision
</idea>

## Selected Approach: **Cinematic Dark Editorial** (Response 2)

The cinematic dark editorial approach best serves CHHS DECA because:
- The deep dark canvas makes the 3D DECA glass truly pop as a centerpiece
- Editorial typography creates the sense of elite achievement and ICDC ambition
- The blue-black color scheme requested by the user is perfectly embodied
- Scroll-driven storytelling keeps students engaged as they explore resources
- The premium feel reinforces DECA's brand as a prestigious organization
