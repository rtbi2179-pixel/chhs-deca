# DECA Exam Structure & Performance Indicators

## Overview
DECA exams are rigorous, industry-validated, multiple-choice, 100-item exams based on National Curriculum Standards. Each cluster has specific performance indicators tested across district, association, and international levels.

## Question Format
- **Type**: Multiple choice
- **Options**: 4 choices per question
- **Total Questions**: 100 per exam
- **Time Limit**: Varies by event (typically 120 minutes)
- **Difficulty**: Mix of foundational, intermediate, and advanced concepts

## Question Design Principles
1. Test real-world business scenarios
2. Require application of knowledge, not just memorization
3. Use industry-standard terminology
4. Include realistic business contexts and examples
5. Test decision-making and problem-solving skills
6. Align with current business practices and standards
