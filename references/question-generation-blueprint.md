# DECA Question Generation Blueprint

## Overview
This document outlines the framework for generating 40,000+ DECA-aligned practice questions that replicate the style, terminology, and content distribution of official DECA exams.

## Question Generation Principles

### 1. Authenticity to DECA Standards
- Use DECA's official Performance Indicators (PIs) as the foundation for each question
- Replicate DECA's business terminology and phrasing patterns
- Test real-world business scenarios and decision-making
- Ensure questions require application of knowledge, not just memorization
- Include industry-standard language and concepts

### 2. Content Distribution
Each cluster exam contains 100 questions distributed across multiple instructional areas. Generate questions proportionally to match DECA's official exam blueprints.

### 3. Question Format
- **Type**: Multiple choice
- **Options**: 4 choices per question (A, B, C, D)
- **Correct Answer**: One definitive correct answer
- **Explanation**: Detailed explanation for why the correct answer is right
- **Difficulty**: Mix of foundational, intermediate, and advanced (roughly 30% beginner, 50% intermediate, 20% advanced)

### 4. Cluster-Specific Guidance

#### Marketing Cluster
**Key Performance Indicators to Test:**
- Market segmentation and targeting strategies
- Marketing mix (4 Ps: Product, Price, Place, Promotion)
- Consumer behavior and market research
- Channel management and distribution
- Pricing strategies and elasticity
- Promotional campaigns and advertising
- Brand management and positioning
- Product lifecycle management
- Selling techniques and customer service
- Marketing ethics and regulations (SUGGING, privacy)

**Question Style Examples:**
- "Which of the following best describes market segmentation?" (Concept definition)
- "A company is experiencing declining sales. Which strategy would be most appropriate?" (Scenario-based decision)
- "The marketing mix includes all of the following EXCEPT:" (Exclusion-based)
- "Calculate the market share given..." (Calculation-based)

## Question Generation Process

### Step 1: Cluster Selection
Select a cluster (Marketing, Finance, Business Admin Core, Hospitality, Management, Entrepreneurship, PFL)

### Step 2: Performance Indicator Selection
Choose a specific PI within that cluster

### Step 3: Question Type Selection
Randomly select from:
- Concept/Definition questions (30%)
- Scenario-based decision questions (40%)
- Calculation/Analysis questions (20%)
- Comparison/Exclusion questions (10%)

### Step 4: Question Generation
Generate question following DECA's style and terminology

### Step 5: Answer Generation
Create 4 plausible answers with one correct answer

### Step 6: Explanation Generation
Write detailed explanation for correct answer

### Step 7: Metadata Assignment
- Cluster: [Cluster Name]
- Performance Indicator: [PI Name]
- Difficulty: [Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced]
- Question Type: [Type]

## Quality Assurance Checklist

For each generated question, verify:
- [ ] Uses DECA-appropriate terminology
- [ ] Tests a specific Performance Indicator
- [ ] Has one clear correct answer
- [ ] Includes plausible distractors
- [ ] Explanation is clear and educational
- [ ] Question is at appropriate difficulty level
- [ ] Scenario (if applicable) is realistic
- [ ] No ambiguity in question wording
- [ ] Calculations (if applicable) are accurate
- [ ] Question doesn't repeat content of other questions

## Implementation Notes

1. **Batch Generation**: Generate questions in batches of 100-200 per PI to maintain consistency
2. **Variation**: Ensure questions within the same PI vary in scenario, numbers, and context
3. **Real-World Relevance**: Use contemporary business examples and current industry practices
4. **Inclusivity**: Include diverse business scenarios and industries
5. **Accuracy**: Verify all calculations, formulas, and business concepts are accurate
6. **Alignment**: Cross-reference with DECA's official materials to ensure alignment
